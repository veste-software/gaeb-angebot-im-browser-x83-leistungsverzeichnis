## GAEB reference (from memory; verify against the official XSDs in task 15)

- Root `<GAEB xmlns="http://www.gaeb.de/GAEB_DA_XML/DA83/3.3">` (3.2: `.../DA83/3.2`; 3.1: `.../200407`). X84 uses `DA84` in the namespace.
- `GAEBInfo/Version`, `GAEBInfo/VersDate`; `PrjInfo/NamePrj`, `PrjInfo/LblPrj`, `PrjInfo/Cur`; `Award/DP` (83/84); `Award/OWN/Address/Name1…`; bidder `Award/CTR/Address/Name1, Street, PCode, City`.
- `Award/BoQ[@ID]/BoQInfo/BoQBkdn` (Type `BoQLevel`/`Item`/`Index`, Length) defines the OZ; `BoQBody/BoQCtgy[@ID,@RNoPart]/LblTx` and nested `BoQBody`; items in `BoQBody/Itemlist/Item[@ID,@RNoPart]`.
- Item: `Qty`, `QU`, `Description/CompleteText/OutlineText/OutlTxt/TextOutlTxt` (short text), `.../DetailTxt/Text` (long text), `Provis` (`WithTotal`/`WithoutTotal`), `ALNGroupNo` + `ALNSerNo` (alternatives), `LumpSumItem`, `NotAppl`, Zulage flag, `TextComplement` (bidder complements). X84 adds `UP`, `IT` per item and `Totals/Total` on categories and the BoQ.
