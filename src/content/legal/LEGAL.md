# Legal pages

impressum.*.html and datenschutz.*.html in this folder are written by the studio from the owner's company texts. They are overwritten before every build and review: do not edit them.
Show them unchanged on the routes /impressum and /datenschutz (in Astro: import them with `?raw` and print them with `set:html`), in the language of the site.
Facts about this product that belong in the privacy text go into product-privacy.de.html and product-privacy.en.html in this folder (plain HTML paragraphs, no placeholders).
